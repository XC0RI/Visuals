About

We are an architectural visualization practice operating at the intersection of rendering and image-making, web design, and design consulting. Informed by professional experience in Zürich, Munich, and Aachen, we develop the visual language of architecture and space.

Gate Visual Studio was founded by Kian Matine, based between Zürich and Paris — with experience across architectural competition, visualization, scenography, and web design, and a drive toward new forms of spatial and digital expression.
